Laboratory

E3M

The Laboratory of Energy Economics and Environmental Management

E3M was founded in 2017 at City University of Hong Kong and is the first laboratory in Hong Kong dedicated to the economic analysis of energy and environmental issues. The lab studies the interactions between the economy, energy, and the environment, aiming to build a sustainable and smart-growth framework for economic development in an era of global change. I joined the laboratory in 2023 as a joint Ph.D. candidate (CityU–UCAS).
