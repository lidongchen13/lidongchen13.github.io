Lidong Chen

PhD Candidate in Economics

City University of Hong Kong · University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the joint doctoral program between the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and City University of Hong Kong. My research focuses on environmental and energy economics, with a strong emphasis on applied microeconomics. My primary interests lie in household economic decision-making and welfare analysis in the context of energy transitions. I also study firm-level decision-making under uncertainty. My research agenda integrates survey data and big data with causal inference methods to understand household and firm behavior in energy transitions, and to examine the environmental, energy, and sustainability challenges arising from broader economic transformation.
